# Safaric Consulting

Safaric Consulting is a management consultancy specializing in business and IT transformation, with a particular focus on retail, wholesale, and consumer-facing industries.

We support our clients in developing and implementing strategies, optimizing business processes, designing future operating models, and leveraging technology, data, and artificial intelligence to improve business performance.

Our areas of expertise include:

- Business and IT strategy
- Digital transformation and process optimization
- Data, analytics, and artificial intelligence
- AI-enabled process solutions and automation
- IT architecture and software solutions
- Responsible and secure use of AI

In addition to our consulting activities, we develop and maintain software and AI solutions that support business processes transformation.
