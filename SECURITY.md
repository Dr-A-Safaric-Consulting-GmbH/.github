# Security Policy

## Security at Safaric Consulting

Safaric Consulting develops and maintains internal software and AI solutions alongside its management consulting activities.

We are committed to the responsible and secure development and use of these solutions.

## Reporting a security issue

If you believe you have identified a security issue affecting software or AI solutions developed or maintained by Safaric Consulting, please contact us privately.
